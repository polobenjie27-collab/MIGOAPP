# MIGO: On-Demand Motorcycle Ride-Hailing & Delivery Platform

## Project Description
**MIGO** (inspired by the Visayan term for *friend*) is an on-demand motorcycle taxi and delivery booking mobile application similar to Angkas. It is designed to connect daily commuters and local businesses with verified, safety-trained motorcycle riders to navigate urban traffic efficiently, safely, and affordably.

---

## Problem Being Addressed
Rapid urbanization and growing vehicle volume have led to severe traffic congestion, making daily commutes unpredictable and time-consuming. Commuters often struggle with:
* **Long Transit Times:** Traditional four-wheeled public transportation gets easily gridlocked during peak hours.
* **Lack of Safety and Standardization:** Informal motorcycle taxis (*habal-habal*) often lack standardized fare matrices, rider background checks, safety gear, and insurance coverage.
* **Inefficient Last-Mile Logistics:** Individuals and small businesses need fast, affordable, same-day parcel delivery across congested city streets.

---

## Target Users / Stakeholders
1. **Commuters / Passengers:** Students, office workers, and daily travelers seeking a fast, reliable, and cost-effective mode of transportation to beat city traffic.
2. **Partner Riders (Drivers):** Licensed motorcycle owners looking for flexible, sustainable income opportunities through passenger transport and parcel delivery.
3. **Local Merchants & Senders:** Small-to-medium enterprises (SMEs) and individuals needing rapid point-to-point courier services.
4. **System Administrators:** Operations and safety personnel responsible for rider verification, fare management, dispute resolution, and platform monitoring.

---

## System Objectives
* **Reduce Commute Times:** Provide a fast, agile alternative to conventional public transit through real-time GPS matching.
* **Ensure Passenger and Rider Safety:** Enforce strict rider onboarding (license verification, safety training), provide safety gear guidelines, and integrate live trip-sharing and SOS features.
* **Establish Transparent Pricing:** Eliminate fare haggling by implementing an automated, distance-based fare calculation system.
* **Empower Local Riders:** Offer a user-friendly driver interface with transparent earnings tracking and flexible working hours.

---

## Major Features / Modules

### 1. User & Rider Authentication Module
* Account registration and login via mobile number/OTP or email.
* **KYC (Know Your Customer) Verification** for riders (driver’s license, OR/CR, NBI/Police clearance, and safety assessment records).

### 2. Booking & Real-Time GPS Tracking Module
* Interactive map interface for setting pickup and drop-off locations.
* Automated rider-passenger matching algorithm based on proximity.
* Live turn-by-turn navigation and real-time location tracking for both parties.

### 3. Automated Fare Estimation & Payment Module
* Upfront fare calculation based on distance, base rate, and traffic conditions.
* Support for cash payments and cashless transactions (e-wallets, in-app wallet).

### 4. Parcel Delivery (*MIGO Padala*) Module
* Dedicated workflow for point-to-point package delivery.
* Sender/recipient details input, item description, and proof-of-delivery (photo/signature) confirmation.

### 5. Safety & Emergency Module
* In-app SOS emergency button linked to local emergency contacts and platform support.
* Shareable live trip links so family or friends can monitor the ride in real time.

### 6. Rating, Review & History Module
* Two-way rating system (1–5 stars) and feedback after every completed trip.
* Comprehensive trip and transaction history logs for both passengers and riders.

### 7. Admin Management Dashboard
* Web-based portal for approving rider applications, monitoring active rides, configuring base fares, and handling user reports.
